About the studio

We rebuilt the agency. Senior only, and half the invoice.

Elite Content is a small studio of senior writers, editors, and SEO strategists who got tired of watching the agency model bill clients for org charts instead of work.

Our story

Founded in a Slack DM about a $40k retainer.

In 2022, three of us — a senior editor, an SEO lead, and a founder ghostwriter — were all freelancing for the same fintech. They were paying a name-brand agency $40,000 a month for content that we were quietly rewriting on the side.

The math was absurd. We started Elite Content to fix it. Same caliber of writing. Senior brains on every brief. None of the seven-person account teams or quarterly strategy theatre.

Three years later we work with a small bench of clients across B2B SaaS, fintech, cybersecurity, and health. We turn down more work than we take. We bill, on average, about half what comparable agencies bill — and our clients see better results.

What we believe

Six principles. They're on the wall, and in every brief.

i.

Senior writers, every time.

No junior bench, no "AI-assisted" filler. If our name is on the studio wall, our brain is on your brief.

ii.

Strategy is not a deck.

It's a one-page map of what to write, why, and how it'll perform. We send the map with the work, not before it.

iii.

Editorial taste compounds.

Sentence rhythm, structure, and the line where the reader decides to keep scrolling. We sweat the parts most agencies skip.

iv.

Bill the work, not the org chart.

No account managers. No layers between you and your writer. The writer hits send. The writer gets the reply.

v.

Earn the next month.

Month-to-month, always. If a quarter goes by and we haven't moved a number, you shouldn't be locked in.

vi.

Say the thing.

We won't write hedged, opinion-free filler that could've been pulled from any of your competitors. If we believe it, we write it.
