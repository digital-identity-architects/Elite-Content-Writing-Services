Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Elite Content Studio ("Elite Content," "we," "our," or "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you visit elitecontent.studio (the "Site"), contact us, or engage our services.

We try to handle personal information the way we'd want our own handled: collect as little as possible, use it only for what you'd reasonably expect, and never sell it.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you give us

  • Contact details when you fill in the contact form or email us — name, company, work email, website, and the message you send.
  • Newsletter subscription — your email address only.
  • Project and engagement information — anything you choose to share during a diagnostic call or paid engagement, including documents and materials needed to do the work.
  • Billing information — invoicing details for clients (collected and stored by our payment processor; we do not store full card numbers).

1.2 Information we collect automatically

  • Standard server logs — IP address, browser type, referring page, and timestamps. We use these for security and to keep the Site running.
  • Privacy-respecting analytics — aggregated, anonymized page views without cross-site tracking or personally identifiable identifiers.

1.3 What we don't collect

We don't use third-party advertising trackers, social-media pixels, or behavioral ad-targeting cookies. We don't fingerprint visitors. We don't sell or rent personal information to anyone.

2. How we use information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Reply to your inquiries and schedule diagnostic calls.
  • Deliver and improve our services for paying clients.
  • Send our monthly newsletter to subscribers (and only to subscribers).
  • Operate, maintain, and secure the Site.
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our Terms.

3. Legal bases (for visitors in the EU/UK)

If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on the following legal bases under the GDPR:

  • Contract — to provide services you've engaged us for.
  • Consent — for newsletter subscriptions; you can withdraw at any time.
  • Legitimate interests — to operate the Site, secure it, respond to inquiries, and improve our services, balanced against your rights.
  • Legal obligation — where applicable.

4. Sharing & disclosures

We share personal information only with:

  • Service providers who help us operate — our email host, hosting provider, payment processor, and analytics tool. Each is contractually bound to handle data per our instructions.
  • Legal recipients when required to comply with law, valid legal process, or to protect rights and safety.
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, subject to confidentiality protections at least as strong as those in this Policy.

We do not sell your personal information. We never have, and we do not plan to.

5. Cookies

The Site uses a small number of strictly necessary cookies for things like remembering mobile-menu state and keeping the Site secure. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings.

6. Data retention

  • Contact form submissions: retained for up to 24 months unless you become a client.
  • Newsletter subscribers: retained until you unsubscribe.
  • Client records and engagement materials: retained for 7 years to meet tax and legal obligations.
  • Server logs: rotated within 30 days unless needed for security investigations.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete information, subject to legal exceptions.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing.
  • Receive your data in a portable format.
  • Withdraw consent (e.g. unsubscribe from the newsletter).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To exercise any of these, email us at privacy@elitecontent.studio and we'll respond within 30 days.

8. International transfers

Our team and service providers operate across the United States, the United Kingdom, and the European Union. Where personal data crosses borders, we use Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards as required by applicable law.

9. Security

We use commercially reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect personal information — encryption in transit, access controls, two-factor authentication on all internal systems, and a small number of vetted vendors. No system is 100% secure, but we treat personal data with the same caution we treat client confidentiality.

10. Children

The Site and our services are not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated by a banner on the Site or, where appropriate, by email.

12. Contact

Questions, requests, or complaints? Email privacy@elitecontent.studio. For data protection matters, address correspondence to "Privacy" — Elite Content Studio.
